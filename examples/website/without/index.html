Heat. Harbour. Cold water.

A sauna and cold-plunge club on a converted barge in Copenhagen harbour. Members book sessions online and step straight from the steam into the sea.

Book a sessionThe ritual

Three rounds, one barge

Halden was a cargo barge for forty years. Now it holds two wood-fired saunas, a plunge pool fed straight from the harbour, and a quiet deck for the time in between.

Heat

80 to 90 degrees in a cedar-lined sauna. Sit for 10 to 15 minutes, with a löyly pour every quarter hour.

Cold

Plunge into 4 to 8 degree harbour water, by ladder or by the pool. One minute is plenty.

Rest

Wrap up on the open deck with tea and watch the ferries. Then go again.

On board

Two wood-fired saunas

Harbour Sauna for 12, Hold Sauna for 6 with a low, dark ceiling and less talk.

Cold plunge

Open water off the stern, plus a heated-down pool kept at 6 degrees.

The deck

Loungers, blankets, tea, and a view of the Opera and Nyhavn.

Changing rooms

Lockers, showers, and towels and robes included with every session.

Good to know

Where is the barge?

Moored at the harbour in Copenhagen. The exact berth is in your booking confirmation.

What should I bring?

Swimwear. We provide towels, robes and lockers.

Is cold plunging safe?

For most healthy adults, yes. Speak to a doctor if you have heart or blood pressure conditions. Hosts are on deck at all times.

Can I cancel?

Free up to 12 hours before your session.
